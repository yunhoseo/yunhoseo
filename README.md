### Research Interests

- **Multi-Camera Multi-Object Tracking (MC-MOT)** — appearance-free and transformer-based association
- **Digital Twin** for manufacturing and logistics environments
- Sequence modeling for occlusion-robust trajectory prediction
- Real-time perception pipelines bridging vision models and industrial control systems
